Dust motes and embers for comp, rebuilt

MoteField adds floating dust and fire embers to a shot in Nuke from the lighting department's utility pass, with no 3D particle render. This page tracks the rebuild. There is no renderer yet: what you can try below is the new settings panel and the shot check.

This update · 5 November 2026

  • Reads production utility passes. Multi-part EXRs, with position, depth, unfiltered depth, coverage, motion vectors and object IDs found by name, across several files a frame (a mattes pass for surface colour and ID mattes).
  • Fixes edges. Filtered passes store edge pixels premultiplied, so their positions streak toward the world origin. They are detected and divided by coverage: on a production pass, edge pixels went from 507 px off to 0.27 px.
  • Reads the whole lens. Blade count, cat-eye, field curvature and full aperture now come from the render camera in the header, so the dust's bokeh can match the plate. 0.8.3 missed them.
  • The shot check. One report on what is in a pass and what MoteField will do with it (below).

The new settings panel

Built from the settings table, as the Nuke node and the viewer will be. Four dials stay on screen, the tabs follow the order you work in, and each tab shows its basic settings until you ask for all of them. The tag on each setting says what changing it costs. Change something to see; double-click a setting's name to reset it.
